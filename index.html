Bio

Who

Dhivya is a Security Leader at Amazon Web Services, where she leads a team of 10+ security engineers and penetration testers responsible for AI and developer-facing products. Her leadership covers the end-to-end security lifecycle for AWS's fastest-moving product lines. To accelerate the SDLC, she oversaw the development of security tooling that enables engineers to scale the creation of custom threat models and design reviews, and implements automated code remediation verification.

Her security career spans more than a decade, including foundational roles at Adobe and Lyft and graduate research at Carnegie Mellon University (MS in Information Technology - Information Security). She currently focuses on AI Safety with Heron AI Security, specifically reproducible purple teaming in agentic environments. Independently, her research explores Evidence-Gated Decision Models for Automated Security Verification and the systemic vulnerabilities emerging from AI-generated code.

She is a regular speaker in the security community, with talks at DEF CON Blue Team Village, BSides Las Vegas, OWASP AppSec DC and California, DefendCon and LocoMocoSec.

Research

Evidence

Work at the intersection of application security and machine learning, with an emphasis on measurable claims. Full list on Google Scholar.

Date Paper Summary
Jul 2026 SecDrift: Measuring Sector-Conditioned Security Drift in AI-Generated Code Preprint with Natraj Bharadwaj. A benchmark of vulnerability rates in LLM-generated code across 8 CISA sectors, 9 CWE categories and 7 models (5,355 evaluations). Central finding: apparent protective drift by sector is a composition artifact; model selection dominates outcomes.
2025 ACSE-Eval: Can LLMs Threat Model Real-World Cloud Infrastructure? with Munshi, Pathak, Ghatode, Priyadarshini and Rana. A dataset of 100 production-grade AWS deployment scenarios with IaC implementations and documented vulnerabilities, used to evaluate LLM threat modeling. Published as a Springer chapter (2026).
